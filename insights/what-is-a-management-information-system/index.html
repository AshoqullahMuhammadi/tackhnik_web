What is a management information system?

A practical guide to the role of an MIS in connecting operations, information, and decisions.

Published and updated September 5, 2026

A management information system, or MIS, organizes operational information so the right people can record work, understand what is happening, and make informed decisions.

A useful MIS is more than a dashboard. It connects the workflow that creates data with the reports, permissions, and controls that make the data trustworthy.

Custom development becomes valuable when an organization has distinct approvals, roles, integrations, or reporting requirements that generic software cannot represent without extensive workarounds.
