Workflow design

Map the workflow before you automate it

How to find the real decisions, exceptions, and handoffs before turning a process into software.

Published and updated September 5, 2026

Automation works best after the current process is understood. Start with the trigger, the people involved, the information they need, the decisions they make, and the exceptions they handle.

Do not copy every manual step into software. Separate controls that protect the business from habits that only exist because the current tools are limited.

The result should be a shared process map and a small set of measurable outcomes. That gives design and engineering a reliable basis for scope.
