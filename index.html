Common questions

Clear answers before we begin.

What kinds of software does TAKHNIK build?

We design custom business platforms, management information systems, workflow automation, integrations, and web and mobile applications.

Can TAKHNIK build a custom MIS for our organization?

Yes. We begin by mapping your workflows, information, roles, reporting needs, and existing systems before defining a suitable MIS.

Do you develop both web and mobile applications?

Yes. The right delivery approach depends on who will use the product, where they work, and which device capabilities they need.

Can you integrate with our existing software and APIs?

Yes. We can design dependable data flows around available APIs, databases, and phased modernization constraints.

How does a software project begin?

A project begins with focused discovery: the problem, users, workflow, constraints, risks, and the outcome the software must improve.

Can you modernize an existing system?

Yes. We assess what should remain, what creates risk, and how to modernize in stages without losing valuable operational knowledge.
